# THE TEMPLATE

INT. OPEN PLAN OFFICE - MORNING

Fluorescent hum. BRENT approaches PAUL's desk holding a coffee mug he never drinks from.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Morning, Paul.

PAUL
(flat)
Morning.

BRENT
Yeah, listen. Did you get a chance to send through that Q3 rollup?

PAUL
Friday. I sent it Friday.

BRENT
Right. Right, you did.

[BEAT 1200]

BRENT
So if you could go ahead and send it again, that'd be terrific.

PAUL
(confused)
Send the same file.

BRENT
The same file, yeah. In the new template.

PAUL
(flat)
There's a new template.

BRENT
(deadpan)
There's a new template.

[BEAT 1500]

PAUL
Since when.

BRENT
Thursday.

PAUL
(flat)
I sent it Friday.

BRENT
(smug)
And that's the thing.

[BEAT 1600]

BRENT
Sharon's just gonna need the header in Corporate Blue.

PAUL
It is blue.

BRENT
It's a blue. It's not Corporate Blue.

[BEAT 1100]

PAUL
(defeated)
What's the difference.

BRENT
(deadpan)
About four hours.

[BEAT 1800]

BRENT
So that'd be great.

Brent taps the desk twice and wanders off.

PAUL
(flat)
Yeah.
