// Office Space register: passive-aggressive management, process as religion.
// The engine of the joke is recursion — a meeting whose only agenda item is to
// circle back, which generates another item to circle back on, forever.
// Three-hander, so it exercises SL/CENTER/SR staging.

# THE CIRCLE BACK

INT. CONFERENCE ROOM B - MORNING

Brent enters with a laptop he will not open and a mug he will not drink from.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Morning, Paul. Grab a seat, this'll be quick.

PAUL
(flat)
I'm already sitting.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Then we're ahead of schedule.

[BEAT 1200]

BRENT
(deadpan)
So. We're here to circle back on item four.

PAUL
(confused)
What's item four.

BRENT
(smug)
That's what we're circling back on.

[BEAT 1500]

Janice from Compliance arrives at a level of energy nobody requested.

JANICE
(cheerful)
Hi! Sorry, I was in the pre-meeting.

PAUL
(flat)
There was a pre-meeting.

JANICE
(pleased)
To align on the circle back.

PAUL
(flat)
Align on what.

JANICE
(cheerful)
On the circling.

[BEAT 1600]

PAUL
(annoyed)
Can somebody just tell me what item four is.

BRENT
(deadpan)
It's in the deck.

PAUL
(flat)
Can I see the deck.

BRENT
(deadpan)
We'll circle back on the deck.

[BEAT 1800]

JANICE
(bright)
I pulled the history! Item four's been circled back on thirty-one times.

PAUL
(shocked)
Thirty-one.

JANICE
(pleased)
Since 2019. It's our most consistent item.

[BEAT 1400]

PAUL
(quiet)
Who raised it.

JANICE
(cheerful)
Doug!

PAUL
(flat)
Doug retired.

JANICE
(pleased)
Four years ago. That's why it stays on the agenda. Out of respect.

[BEAT 1800]

PAUL
(annoyed)
Okay. Let's just decide it. Right now. The three of us.

Brent sets down the mug. It is the most alarmed he has been in eleven years.

BRENT
(shocked)
Decide it.

PAUL
(flat)
Decide it.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Paul. This is a circle back. We don't decide in a circle back.

PAUL
(confused)
Then when do we decide.

BRENT
(smug)
In the meeting we circle back to.

[BEAT 2000]

PAUL
(defeated)
And when is that.

BRENT
(deadpan)
We'll circle back on that.

[BEAT 1600]

JANICE
(bright)
Capturing that as item five!

PAUL
(quiet)
Please don't.

JANICE
(pleased)
Already did. We'll circle back on it Thursday.

[BEAT 1500]

Brent stands, taps the table twice, and retrieves the mug.

BRENT
(deadpan)
So if you could go ahead and hold Thursday, that'd be great.

PAUL
(flat)
Nothing happened in this meeting.

BRENT
(smug)
That's why it finished early.

[BEAT 2000]

Brent leaves. Janice does not.

JANICE
(cheerful)
Same time next week!

PAUL
(sad)
For what.

JANICE
(bright)
We'll circle back.

[BEAT 2000]
