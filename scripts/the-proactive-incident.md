# THE PROACTIVE INCIDENT

INT. SOFTWARE TEAM OFFICE - MORNING

VERN sits down at the desk.

MEL enters and moves to center.

MEL
(deadpan)
Good news. The outage dashboard is completely green.

VERN
(flat)
We fixed the outages.

MEL
(confused)
Right. That's the visibility problem.

[BEAT 700]

MEL reaches for the mug.

MEL picks up the mug.

MEL
(smug)
Leadership can no longer see what engineering does.

MEL puts down the mug on the desk and taps the monitor twice.

MEL
(cheerful)
So this sprint, we need one proactive incident.

VERN
(shocked)
You want me to break production.

MEL
(deadpan)
Not break. Create a measurable recovery opportunity.

[BEAT 900]

VERN
(defeated)
What do I write in the postmortem.

MEL
(deadpan)
Insufficient resilience.

VERN
(flat)
It hasn't happened yet.

MEL
(smug)
That's why it's proactive.

[BEAT 1200]

MEL exits.

VERN moves to center and taps the laptop three times.

VERN
(deadpan)
Scheduling incident.
