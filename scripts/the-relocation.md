# THE RELOCATION

INT. OPEN PLAN OFFICE - MORNING

Brent approaches holding a clipboard he has never once written on.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Morning, Paul. Quick one.

PAUL
(flat)
Morning.

BRENT
We're relocating the office three seconds into the past. Effective Monday.

[BEAT 1500]

PAUL
Sorry, what.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Three seconds. Into the past.

PAUL
(confused)
Why would we do that.

BRENT
(smug)
Tax reasons.

[BEAT 1400]

PAUL
(flat)
The office is moving backwards through time.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Not backwards. It's a lateral move.

Janice from Compliance arrives at a speed nobody asked for.

JANICE
(cheerful)
Hi! I'm just here to make sure everybody's excited.

PAUL
(flat)
I have questions.

JANICE
(pleased)
That's so valid.

[BEAT 1200]

PAUL
Will I still get paid on the first?

JANICE
(cheerful)
You already have. Twice, actually. We're recovering one of them.

[BEAT 1600]

PAUL
(defeated)
Which one.

BRENT
(deadpan)
That's above my level.

JANICE
(pleased)
It's above everybody's level. That's what makes it a policy.

[BEAT 1500]

PAUL
(quiet)
And if I say no.

BRENT
(deadpan)
Then you'll have said no on Thursday.

[BEAT 1800]

BRENT
So that'd be great.

Brent taps the desk twice and wanders off. Janice does not leave.

JANICE
(cheerful)
I'll be here all week. Technically I already was.
